OksaSDR (formerly "AetherSDR Mobile")

Welcome, and thank you for testing! OksaSDR is a FlexRadio client for iPad and iPhone, built on the AetherSDR engine. It talks to your radio directly: on your home network (enter the radio's IP address), from anywhere over SmartLink, or over your own VPN.

GETTING STARTED
- Connect: enter your radio's IP address and tap Connect, or sign in to SmartLink on the Connect page.
- Start with receive: check the spectrum, waterfall and audio.
- [TX] = transmits. Test at low power or into a dummy load first. The app sets the radio's mic input to PC (this device's microphone) when it connects.
- The Manual button (top row) opens the user manual.

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- DX cluster spots: View > Spots connects to a DX cluster (your radio's callsign, or set one); spots show as callsigns on the display, tap one to tune. Spots from other programs on the radio show too.
- Notch filters (TNF): press and hold on the display to put a notch there; tap it for width, depth, permanent or remove. DSP tab: TNF on/off and + TNF.
- TX tab, with this device's microphone: processing before it is sent - gate, EQ (low cut, bass, presence) and compressor. [TX]
- Memories: press and hold one to edit its name, frequency and mode.
- Voice keyer: Listen plays a recording back to you without transmitting.
- The Mic meter now moves with this device's microphone while you transmit (it stayed at the bottom).

RECENT
- Radios on the connect page are clearly buttons; the pan control reads C, L or R and snaps to centre.
- iPhone: the controls open from the button beside PTT; the drawer reaches the bottom edge.
- View: FFT frames/s and waterfall speed keep the value you set.
- New app icon; volume and pan sliders follow your finger; meter peak hold fixed.
- Simpler TX tab: MOX removed (hold PTT), one Mic level. The callsign is set in Setup > Radio.

PLEASE TRY
- Receive on a few bands, with zoom, the Band button and the 3D view.
- Your connection type: home network, SmartLink or VPN. Disconnect and reconnect; the spectrum and audio should come back.
- Both portrait and landscape; on iPad also Split View.
- Voice transmit with the device mic or a Bluetooth headset, and tell me how it sounds at the other end. [TX]
- CW keying, CWX memories (F1–F12 on a keyboard) and the decoder. [TX]
- New: spots and the DX cluster, a notch on a carrier, the TX processing at low power with a second receiver. [TX]

REPORTING A PROBLEM
Right after it happens, open Log in the top row, tap Copy, and paste the text into an email to oh6oksa@gmail.com. Add your device model, radio model and what you were doing. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
